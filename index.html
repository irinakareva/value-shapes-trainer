Reference ideas, prompts, and what makes a good image

Usually works well

  • 3-6 big value masses still read when you squint.
  • Clear side lighting or one obvious light source.
  • Simple background and one main subject.
  • Large overlapping forms rather than tiny details.
  • Portraits with simple lighting.
  • Figures with a readable gesture and strong silhouette.

Usually less helpful

  • Busy patterns, foliage, crowds, text-heavy screenshots.
  • Very flat lighting.
  • Multiple competing light sources.
  • Images that depend on tiny details.
  • Noisy backgrounds or patterned clothing.
